Suomi-treeni (English version)

A Finnish learning app with English translations: phrase training with spaced repetition, role-play dialogues, verb conjugation, numbers by ear, reading texts and preparation for the YKI exam (intermediate level).

https://olleksa.github.io/Suomi-treeni-eng/

It runs in the browser, so it works on any device: a computer, iPhone, iPad and Android phone. You can add it to your home screen, and it works without internet.

How to add it to your home screen

iPhone and iPad
Open the link to the app in Safari.
Tap Share — the square with an arrow pointing up (at the bottom of the screen on iPhone, at the top on iPad). If you can't see the button, tap … in the address bar.
Scroll down the menu and choose Add to Home Screen.
Tap Add. The icon will appear on your home screen — open the app from it.

Android (Chrome)
Open the link in Chrome.
Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
Choose Install app (or Add to Home screen) and confirm.
